# 02.5 library subgraph: read side

## Goal
A `library` subgraph on port 4003 that stores Reading Statuses, Custom Shelves and Reviews in SQLite and serves every read field your design gives it.

## Notes
- Tables (adjust to your design): `reading_status(user_id, book_id, status, updated_at)` with a unique key on (user, book); `shelf(id, user_id, name, created_at)` with a unique key on (user, name); `shelf_book(shelf_id, book_id, added_at)`; `review(id, user_id, book_id, rating, text, created_at, updated_at)` with a unique key on (user, book).
- `user_id` and `book_id` are plain strings: there are **no foreign keys across subgraphs**. That's an important property of federation; think about what it means for consistency.
- Status Shelves are derived from `reading_status` at read time (ADR 0002). Give them the stable ids you chose in 01.1.
- `averageRating` is computed in SQL (`AVG(rating)`), and is `null` when there are no Reviews.
- Implement every reference resolver and contributed field your entity map assigns to `library`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] With rows inserted by a test, the subgraph resolves a User's Shelves (3 Status Shelves + Custom Shelves) with Books in newest-added order.
- [ ] `averageRating` for a Book with Ratings 4 and 5 is `4.5`; with no Reviews it's `null`.
- [ ] A Book on two Custom Shelves and with a Reading Status shows up in all three places.

## Concepts practised
Contributing fields to entities you don't own · derived data (Status Shelves) · data ownership without foreign keys
